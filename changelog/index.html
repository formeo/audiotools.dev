Changelog

What's new on AudioTools.dev, newest first. The full history is on GitHub.

October 22, 2026

  • Several files at once in the Audio Converter and the Audio Compressor: drop a batch, follow each file's progress, download them one by one or all as a ZIP.
  • Audio Converter saves M4A (AAC) and FLAC as well as MP3 and WAV, and takes video files (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM) to pull out the sound.
  • The homepage helper understands settings: “compress to 10 MB”, “keep the first 30 seconds”, “1.5x faster”, “louder by 6 dB”, “convert to FLAC” (also in Russian and Chinese). It opens the tool with your file and those settings already in place.
  • Large files: the converter now reads files in pieces instead of loading them whole, so long recordings and videos work.
  • A new engine under the hood: Mediabunny, served from this site, replaces the old lamejs MP3 encoder on every tool. MP3 encoding runs in the background, so the page no longer freezes on long files.

October 20, 2026

  • New link previews for each tool in messengers and social networks, and a new site icon.
  • Larger tap targets in the menu and footer on phones.

October 19, 2026

  • A new light design across the whole site, with the same navigation, footer, headers and buttons everywhere.
  • Homepage helper: drop a file or describe what you need, and it suggests the right tool and opens it with your file.
  • Trimmer, Joiner, Volume Booster and Speed Changer can now save as MP3 as well as WAV.
  • After a result, “Continue with this file” sends it straight to the next tool.
  • Audio Converter no longer offers FLAC output: it needed a WebAssembly module that was never deployed, so the option never worked. MP3 and WAV output are unchanged, and FLAC files can still be opened.
  • Audio Converter in Chinese at /zh/converter/.
  • This changelog and the About page.

October 13, 2026

  • Voice to Text: Private mode transcribes audio files and recordings with the Whisper model running on your device.

October 12, 2026

  • Clearer privacy wording: the site now says exactly what it loads, including anonymous page analytics.

September 10, 2026

  • Homepage in Chinese at /zh/.

July 16, 2026

  • Audio Joiner: merge several files into one, with optional crossfade.
  • Audio Compressor: make files smaller by choosing MP3 bitrate and sample rate.

June 29, 2026

  • Speed Changer: 0.25× to 4× without changing pitch (WSOLA time-stretching).
  • Volume Booster: boost up to 500% or normalize, with a soft limiter against clipping.

February 19, 2026

  • Audio Trimmer with a visual waveform editor.
  • Text to Speech in 50+ languages.

February 10, 2026

  • Audio Converter: WAV, MP3 and FLAC output.
  • Privacy policy and terms of use.

February 9, 2026

  • Voice to Text: live dictation in 60+ languages.

January 13, 2026

  • AudioTools.dev goes live as the home of the open-source audio projects.
