Vocal Remover

Take the voice out of a song and keep the music — on your device, without uploading the song

Coming soon

What it will do

  • Split a song into two files: the instrumental (a backing or karaoke track) and the vocals on their own.
  • Run in your browser with an open-source separation model, like every tool here: the song is never uploaded.
  • Open MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC files, and the sound of a video.

There is no date yet. New tools are announced in the changelog.

Learning a song in the meantime? Slow it down without changing the pitch, or cut out the part you practise with the audio trimmer.
