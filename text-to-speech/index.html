Free Online Text to Speech Converter

AudioTools Text to Speech converts any written text into natural-sounding speech directly in your browser. It uses the Web Speech Synthesis API — a built-in browser technology that provides high-quality voices in dozens of languages without requiring any server or internet connection for processing.

How It Works

Type or paste your text, select a voice and language, adjust speed and pitch, then click "Speak". The browser's speech engine processes your text locally and plays it through your speakers. Your text is never sent to any server — everything happens on your device.

Supported Languages

Available voices depend on your browser and operating system. Most modern browsers provide 50+ voices covering major world languages including English (US, UK, Australian), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese (Mandarin, Cantonese), Korean, Hindi, Arabic, and many more.

Use Cases

  • Proofreading — hear your writing to catch errors your eyes miss
  • Language learning — listen to proper pronunciation in any language
  • Accessibility — convert articles and documents to audio
  • Content creation — preview voiceover scripts before recording
  • Multitasking — listen to text while doing other work

Privacy & Security

All text-to-speech processing happens in your browser using the Web Speech Synthesis API. Your text never leaves your device — there are no servers, no APIs, no data collection. This is the same technology your browser uses for its built-in read-aloud features.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this text-to-speech tool free?
Yes, completely free with no limits. It uses your browser's built-in Speech Synthesis API, so there are no server costs and no restrictions on usage.
Can I download the speech as an audio file?
The Web Speech Synthesis API plays audio directly through your speakers. For downloadable audio, you can use screen recording software to capture the output. We may add a download feature in a future update.
Which voices and languages are available?
Available voices depend on your browser and operating system. Chrome typically offers the most voices (50+), followed by Safari and Edge. Windows, macOS, and Android each provide their own set of system voices.
Is my text sent to a server?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
