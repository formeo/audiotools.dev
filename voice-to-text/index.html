Free Online Voice to Text Transcription

Audio Tools Voice to Text is a free online speech-to-text transcription tool that converts your spoken words into written text in real-time using your browser's Web Speech Recognition API — no software installation and no account registration. Private mode transcribes audio files and recordings with the Whisper speech model running entirely in your browser, so the audio is never uploaded anywhere.

How It Works

Simply select your language, click "Start Recording", and speak into your microphone. The tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition engine to transcribe your speech in real-time. You can see the text appearing as you speak, and when you're done, copy it to your clipboard or download it as a text file.

Supported Languages

The Voice to Text tool supports over 60 languages and regional dialects, including English (US/UK), Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and many more European and Asian languages.

Use Cases

  • Taking notes during meetings and lectures
  • Dictating emails, messages, and documents
  • Creating subtitles and captions for videos
  • Transcribing interviews and podcasts
  • Accessibility — hands-free text input
  • Language learning and pronunciation practice

Privacy & Security

Live dictation uses your browser's speech recognition engine, which may send audio to the browser vendor (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Private mode runs the Whisper model on your device: the model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, and your audio never leaves the browser. AudioTools.dev has no backend — we never receive your audio or transcriptions.

Browser Compatibility

Voice to Text works best in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. These browsers have built-in support for the Web Speech Recognition API. Firefox has limited support for speech recognition. For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of Chrome or Edge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my voice data sent to a server?

The Web Speech API requires an internet connection and sends audio to the browser vendor (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari) for processing. Your audio is not stored on our servers — we have no backend. In Private mode, speech is transcribed by the Whisper model running in your browser, so your audio never leaves your device.

Which languages are supported?

Over 60 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, and many more. The available languages depend on your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Voice to text works on Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS. Tap the microphone button and speak — results appear in real-time.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on your browser's speech recognition engine. Chrome (powered by Google) provides the best accuracy for most languages. Clear speech in a quiet environment gives the best results.
