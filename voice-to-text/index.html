Transcribe audio to text for free

Voice to Text turns speech into text in two ways. Private mode transcribes audio files and recordings with the Whisper speech model running in your browser, so the audio never leaves your device. Live dictation writes down what you say in real time using your browser's speech engine. Both are free, with no account and no length limit.

How to transcribe an audio file

  1. Choose the spoken language at the top.
  2. In Private mode, pick a model: Base is faster, Small is more accurate. On a computer with WebGPU, Turbo gives the best quality.
  3. Click “Transcribe file” and choose an audio or video file (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG/Opus voice messages, MP4…), or click “Record & transcribe” to use the microphone.
  4. The first time, the model downloads once (80–250 MB; Turbo about 1.6 GB) and is then kept by your browser.
  5. Copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

Voice messages to text

Save a voice message from WhatsApp or Telegram (an .opus or .ogg file) and open it with “Transcribe file”. It is transcribed on your device, which matters for private conversations.

Private mode or live dictation?

  • Private mode — for files and recordings. Runs on your device and works offline once the model is downloaded. Long recordings take a while on slower phones.
  • Live dictation — text appears as you speak. Uses your browser's speech engine, which in Chrome and Safari may send the audio to Google or Apple, and needs an internet connection.

Supported languages

Whisper understands about 100 languages, including English, Chinese, Spanish, Russian, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and Portuguese. Live dictation supports 60+ languages and regional variants; the exact list depends on your browser.

Use cases

  • Notes from meetings, lectures and interviews
  • Voice messages you would rather read
  • Dictating emails, messages and documents
  • A first draft of subtitles for a video
  • Hands-free text input

Privacy

Private mode runs the Whisper model on your device: the model is downloaded once from Hugging Face, and your audio never leaves the browser. Live dictation uses the browser's speech engine, which may send audio to the browser vendor. AudioTools.dev has no backend — we never receive your audio or transcriptions.

Browser compatibility

Private mode works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Live dictation works in Chrome, Edge and Safari; Firefox does not support it.

Need the reverse? Text to speech reads text aloud. To get the sound out of a video or shrink a long recording first, use the audio converter or the audio compressor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a voice recording to text?

Choose the language, click “Transcribe file” in Private mode and pick the recording. Whisper transcribes it in your browser, then you can copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

Can I transcribe WhatsApp or Telegram voice messages?

Yes. Save the voice message from the chat (an .opus or .ogg file) and open it with “Transcribe file”. It is transcribed on your device and never uploaded.

Is my voice data sent to a server?

Not in Private mode: Whisper runs in your browser and your audio never leaves your device. Live dictation uses the browser's speech engine, which in Chrome and Safari sends audio to Google or Apple. AudioTools.dev has no backend and stores nothing.

Is it free? Is there a length limit?

It is free with no limits. Long recordings simply take longer, depending on your device and the model you choose.

Which languages are supported?

Private mode (Whisper) understands about 100 languages. Live dictation supports 60+ languages; the list depends on your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Both modes work in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. Private mode downloads the model once (80–250 MB), so use Wi-Fi the first time.

How accurate is the transcription?

For clear speech, Base is good enough for notes and drafts. Small makes noticeably fewer mistakes, especially in languages other than English, and Turbo (on a computer with WebGPU) is the most accurate. Background noise, music and several people talking at once lower accuracy.
