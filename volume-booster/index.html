Free Online Volume Booster & Audio Normalizer

Volume Booster lets you increase the volume of quiet audio files or normalize audio to a consistent level. Drop a quiet recording, boost the volume or normalize it, and download the result — all in your browser without any server.

Volume Boost vs Normalization

Volume boost increases the entire signal by a fixed amount (for example +6 dB or 200%). Normalization analyzes the peak level and scales the volume so the loudest part reaches a target level (−1 dBFS here). Normalization is smarter — it prevents clipping while maximizing perceived loudness. When you boost manually past the ceiling, a soft limiter rounds off the peaks so the result stays clean instead of crackling.

How It Works

Drop or select your file, pick Normalize or Manual Boost, and click "Apply & Process". The audio is decoded with the Web Audio API, gain is applied sample-by-sample directly in your browser, and the result is offered as a downloadable WAV. Nothing is uploaded.

Common Use Cases

Boosting quiet voice recordings from phone or laptop microphones. Normalizing podcast episodes to a consistent volume. Matching volume levels across multiple tracks. Making quiet audiobook chapters louder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I boost audio volume?
Up to 500% (5x). For best results use Normalize, which finds the optimal level without clipping.
What is audio normalization?
It analyzes the peak volume and scales the file so the loudest part reaches a target level (−1 dBFS), maximizing loudness without distortion.
Is my audio uploaded?
No. Everything happens in your browser using the Web Audio API.
