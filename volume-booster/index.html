Free Online Volume Booster & Audio Normalizer

Volume Booster lets you increase the volume of quiet audio files or normalize audio to a consistent level. Upload a quiet recording, boost the volume, and download the result — all in your browser without any server.

Volume Boost vs Normalization

Volume boost increases the entire signal by a fixed amount (e.g., +6dB or 200%). Normalization analyzes the peak level and adjusts the volume so the loudest part reaches a target level (usually -1dB). Normalization is smarter — it prevents clipping while maximizing perceived loudness.

Common Use Cases

Boosting quiet voice recordings from phone or laptop microphones. Normalizing podcast episodes to consistent volume. Matching volume levels across multiple tracks. Making quiet audiobook chapters louder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I boost audio volume?
Up to 500% (5x) increase. For best results, use normalization which automatically finds the optimal level.
What is audio normalization?
Normalization analyzes the peak volume and adjusts it to a target level, maximizing loudness without clipping or distortion.
Is my audio uploaded?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser using Web Audio API.
